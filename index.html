Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the best way to start an interior design project? +
Begin with a clear brief: how you want to feel in the space, your budget, and non-negotiables. Collect visual references and measure everything before buying a single item.
How do I choose a paint colour that works with my light? +
Test large swatches on multiple walls and observe them at different times of day. North-facing rooms need warmer undertones; south-facing rooms can handle cooler shades.
What’s the ideal ceiling height for a modern home? +
There’s no single ideal, but 2.7–3.0 metres is comfortable for most living areas. Higher ceilings feel grander but require careful lighting and acoustics.
Are open-plan layouts still popular? +
Yes, but with more nuance. Zoning through furniture, partial walls, and ceiling changes keeps openness while adding definition.
