We believe good design is quiet, considered, and deeply human.

Grandscale Home Thinking is an independent journal for people who want to understand the why behind beautiful, functional spaces.

Founded in 2021, we publish in-depth essays, practical guides, and project tours that bridge the gap between professional knowledge and everyday living. No trends for trends' sake — just enduring ideas, tested materials, and the wisdom of making a home that lasts.

What we stand for

Depth over speed

We take time to research, interview, and test. Every article is a considered piece — not a listicle churned out for clicks. We’d rather publish one great guide a week than five shallow ones.

Materials that age well

From lime plaster to solid oak, we explore how things are made, how they wear, and why patina is a feature, not a flaw. Good design gets better with time.

Light as a material

We treat lighting as the invisible architecture it is. Our guides help you layer ambient, task, and accent light for warmth, function, and drama.

“Our goal is not to tell you what’s trendy, but to give you the confidence to make choices that feel right for your life — and your space.”
Elena Marchetti Founder & Editor-in-Chief

Our editorial principles

  • Independence: No sponsored content disguised as editorial. Ever.
  • Practicality: Theory is nothing without application — we show you how.
  • Respect for craft: We credit makers, designers, and builders properly.
  • Slow looking: We believe in the value of deep attention to detail.
  • Inclusivity: Good design is for everyone, not just the privileged few.
  • Sustainability: We favour durable, repairable, and low-impact solutions.

The people behind Grandscale

Elena Marchetti

Founder & Editor-in-Chief

Interior designer and writer with 15 years of residential experience. Elena founded Grandscale to share slow, considered design knowledge. She splits her time between Milan and London.

James Chen

Senior Architecture Editor

Architect and educator. James writes about passive design, vernacular building, and the intersection of climate and shelter. He has taught at the Architectural Association.

Maya Lin

Materials & Furniture Editor

Maya is a furniture maker and materials specialist. Her writing focuses on joinery, finishes, and the stories behind the objects we live with. She runs a small workshop in Portland.

Sofia Ramirez

Lighting Contributor

Lighting designer and photographer. Sofia translates the science of light into human terms — helping readers create spaces that feel as good as they look.
