Cookies Policy

We use cookies to make our site work, understand how it’s used, and improve your experience. Here’s exactly what we use and why.

Last updated: 1 April 2025  ·  Effective: 1 April 2025

This Cookies Policy explains how Grandscale Home Thinking ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies (such as web beacons and pixels) on www.grandscalehome.com (the "Site"). It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle your personal data more broadly.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site owners.

Cookies can be "persistent" (they remain on your device until they expire or you delete them) or "session" cookies (they are deleted when you close your browser). They can also be "first-party" (set by us) or "third-party" (set by another domain, such as an analytics provider).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: These are necessary for the Site to function properly. They enable core features such as page navigation, security, and access to protected areas. The Site cannot function without these cookies.
  • Analytics and performance cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with our Site — which pages are most popular, how long people stay, and whether they encounter errors. This information is aggregated and anonymous, and helps us improve our content and layout.
  • Functionality cookies: These remember your preferences (such as language or region) and provide enhanced, personalised features. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.
  • Targeting and advertising cookies: We do not currently serve third-party advertising on our Site. However, if we ever introduce advertising or embedded media, those third parties may set cookies to build a profile of your interests and show relevant content on other sites.

3. Specific Cookies We Use

The table below lists the main cookies currently used on our Site and their purpose. This list may be updated from time to time as we add or remove services.

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
gs_session Essential (first-party) Maintains your session state while you browse the Site, including any cookie consent choices. Session
gs_preferences Functionality (first-party) Stores your display preferences, such as font size or reading mode, if you choose to customise them. 12 months

Note: Third-party cookies are set by domains other than ours and are subject to those third parties’ own privacy and cookie policies. We do not control the data collected by third-party cookies.

4. Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit our Site, you will be shown a cookie banner asking for your consent to set non-essential cookies. You can accept or reject them, and you can change your mind at any time.

Managing cookies through your browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • See what cookies are stored and delete them individually or all at once.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block cookies from particular sites.
  • Block all cookies from being set.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please note that if you block or delete essential cookies, parts of our Site may not function properly. For guidance on how to manage cookies in your specific browser, visit the help pages of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.

Opting out of analytics

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We respect the "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal where your browser sends one, and we will not set non-essential cookies in that case.

5. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other similar technologies:

  • Web beacons (pixels): Tiny transparent images embedded in pages or emails that help us count visits or understand open rates. These do not store information on your device but may work alongside cookies.
  • Local storage: Some parts of our Site may use browser local storage to store preferences or cache data locally on your device. This data is not automatically sent to our servers.

6. Changes to This Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
